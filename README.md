## Alireza Vafisani

I build AI systems that do a whole job and then keep doing it without me standing over them. Mostly agents that read messy input, decide the cases they can decide, and hand the rest to a person with a plain reason. The part I care about is the one most demos skip, knowing whether the thing still works after you change the prompt.

Computer science at the University of Calgary, finishing in December 2026. This summer I was a research assistant on GenVolVis, three agents on one open 31B model that turn one English sentence and a raw CT scan into a 3D rendering. Against a ParaView MCP agent on 23 tasks, judged by Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-5, it did as well or better on 17 and used about a seventh of the tokens.
